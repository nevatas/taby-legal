# Data Deletion Instructions

**Taby** ("the App") stores all user data locally on your device. No data is stored on external servers.

## How to Delete Your Data

If you would like to remove your data, simply **delete the app** from your device. This will permanently remove all notes, tabs, images, videos, and settings associated with the App.
