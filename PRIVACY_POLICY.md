# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 17, 2026

**Taby** ("the App") is developed and maintained by THE COOL COMPANY ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

## Information We Collect

All notes, tabs, images, and settings you create in the App are stored exclusively on your device. We do not have access to your content.

For the purpose of advertising attribution, the App collects limited technical data through a third-party SDK (see "Third-Party Services" below). This data may include:

- Device identifiers (IDFA, when authorized by you)
- IP address
- User agent
- Basic app usage events (app install, app launch)

This data is used solely for measuring the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and is not linked to your notes or personal content.

## Data Storage

- All content (notes, tabs, images, videos) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults.
- We do not operate servers or databases that store your content.
- We do not use cloud synchronization services (such as iCloud or any third-party cloud).

## Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party service:

- **TikTok Business SDK** — used for advertising attribution and campaign measurement. TikTok may collect device identifiers, IP address, and app events as described above. TikTok's use of this data is governed by [TikTok's Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/privacy-policy/en).

The App does not integrate any other third-party services such as analytics tools, crash reporting services, or social media login SDKs.

## Tracking and Your Choices

When you first open the App, you will be asked whether you allow tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, no advertising identifiers (IDFA) will be shared with third parties. You can change this choice at any time in your device Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal content (notes, images, or settings) to any third party. The only data shared with third parties is the limited technical data described above, transmitted to TikTok for advertising attribution purposes.

## Photos and Camera

The App allows you to attach photos from your photo library or camera. These images are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The advertising attribution data described above is collected in the same manner for all users.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the updated "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
